# Prompt construction patterns

Use these blocks as a planning aid. Replace bracketed fields with observations from the actual photos. Do not paste them unchanged.

## Shared identity block

> Use the supplied photographs as identity references for [subject]. Preserve [specific face/fur/hair/product markers], proportions, and recognizable expression. All appearances are the same subject. No identity drift, invented markings, duplicate anatomy, text, or watermark.

Name concrete visual anchors instead of writing only “keep it the same.” Examples: asymmetric white muzzle, green almond-shaped eyes, chipped red paint on the left edge, triangular brass clasp.

## Source Match block

> Create a [ratio] wallpaper grounded in the photographed world. Build the palette from [dominant colors] with [accent]. Retain [light direction/temperature] and [environmental cues]. Use [hero reference] as the focal subject and [support references] for secondary moments. Refine the scene into a single believable composition with a quiet [left/right] desktop-safe area.

## Intelligent Extension block

> Create a [ratio] wallpaper that grows from the source evidence. Transform [visible cue] into [new environmental structure] while preserving [palette anchor] and [mood]. Integrate the subject naturally through consistent scale, shadows, depth, grain, and light. Use a composition clearly different from the source-matched version and leave a quiet [left/right] desktop-safe area.

## Bold Reimagination block

> Reimagine the same recognizable subject as [chosen direction], motivated by [source evidence or subject character]. Shift the setting to [new setting], the palette to [new palette], and the material language to [new materials]. Keep [identity anchors] realistic and stable. Make it visually daring but coherent, with no generic cyberpunk or celestial motifs unless explicitly relevant.

## Negative constraints

Add only constraints that address plausible failures:

- no extra limbs, faces, fingers, tails, ears, products, or duplicate bodies
- no changed fur pattern, eye color, logo geometry, or facial identity
- no pasted-cutout edges, mismatched perspective, or conflicting light sources
- no text, signature, frame, watermark, or UI mockup
- no clutter in the desktop-safe area

Avoid long generic quality-word chains. Concrete composition and identity instructions are more useful than “masterpiece, best quality, 8K.”
