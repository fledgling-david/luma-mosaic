# Wallpaper quality checklist

Inspect each candidate at full size and as a small thumbnail.

## Identity

- The same person, pet, object, or product is recognisable in every appearance.
- Distinctive markings, eye color, face geometry, proportions, and logos remain stable.
- No merged, missing, or extra anatomy; no accidental duplicate subject.

## Integration

- Light direction, color temperature, shadows, grain, and depth are consistent.
- Edges are clean without halos or obvious pasted cutouts.
- Foreground and background share plausible perspective and scale.

## Composition

- The focal subject reads at thumbnail size.
- The three modes use materially different layouts and scene logic.
- Negative space is intentional and the requested desktop-safe side stays quiet.
- Cropping does not cut important facial or identity features.

## Output

- Aspect ratio and minimum dimensions match the request.
- No unintended text, watermark, signature, border, or interface overlay.
- Files are separate, clearly named, and not near-duplicates.
- Color and contrast remain usable behind desktop icons.

Revise any identity, anatomy, aspect-ratio, or severe integration failure before delivery. Minor texture imperfections may be disclosed when revision would risk losing a stronger identity match.
